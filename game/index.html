HOW TO PLAY

  • On foot: WASD to walk, Shift to sprint, Space to jump. Left click punches.
  • Cars: E enters a parked or stopped car, F carjacks a moving one. W/S throttle and brake, A/D steer, Space handbrake.
  • Camera: drag with the mouse to orbit; it eases back behind the car while you drive. Wheel zooms.
  • Missions: drive to a gold marker to start a job. Follow the objective text and the waypoint on the minimap.
  • Wanted: crimes raise your stars. Stay out of sight to cool down, or clear them at the Spray Shop on the minimap.
  • Combat: 2 draws the pistol (limited ammo, pickups glow around town), 1 returns to fists. Health reaches zero: you wake at the hospital.
  • Side work: taxi fares, the checkpoint time trial, and ten hidden packages around the city.
